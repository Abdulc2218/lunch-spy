HOW TO PLAY LUNCH SPY ===================== * STUDENTS walk around the cafeteria and finish their lunch list. * SPIES do secret missions. A mission looks exactly like a normal task. * After a mission, everyone gets an alert a few seconds later. Who was nearby? * Chat bubbles only reach people CLOSE to you. Walk over to talk. * Ring the LUNCH BELL in the middle of the hall to call a meeting. You can ring it once. * Most votes = DETENTION. You become a ghost and can still do tasks. * Students win: catch every spy or finish all tasks. * Spies win: finish their missions.
